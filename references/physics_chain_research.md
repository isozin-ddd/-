# Physics / Chain reaction research — 2026-11-03

## Result and qualification rule

24 discovery records: 12 physics, 12 chain reaction. **Directly verified original posts with at least 100,000 likes: 0 physics + 0 chain reaction = 0.** These 24 are NOT a completed 20-example high-like collection. Original-post counters remain `null`. Indirect values are stored only in `reported_metrics` with their source URL, historical context and evidence class. `qualifies_100k_likes` is false throughout. Three records have `original_url:null` because only a named example and secondary account of it were discoverable. No fabricated unique post IDs are supplied.

Of the 24 records, 21 have identifiable post URLs; three of those are original Reddit discussion/project posts, another is a Pinterest discovery pin with unknown original creator. References whose original provenance or short-form duration is uncertain are marked explicitly and must not be presented as qualifying Shorts. There are no downloaded third-party videos, images, extracted audio tracks, copied characters or model-training data.

The target of 10 directly verified >=100k-like examples per genre was not met. The evidence does not support a claim of globally representative popularity: creator concentration and accessible English text are substantial biases. Lower-like examples are retained only to show counterexamples and useful mechanics, not to pad the target.

## What was actually observed

Public text descriptions, titles, metadata snippets and creator comments were read. No source video was frame-reviewed. Consequently, individual records leave the first0.5s,1s and3s hooks, sound, exact ending and loop unknown. The `production_parameters` are original proposals to test, never measurements of unseen footage or learned model weights.

The original [Koi Pond Ring Escape Reddit post](https://www.reddit.com/r/blender/comments/1pushx1/satisfying_koi_pond_ring_escape/) has useful author/commenter discussion: the creator describes scripted kinematics; commenters identify apparent wall/ball mismatches. This is practical evidence for testing collision continuity, not a large-like popularity claim. Reddit scores were not converted into likes.
